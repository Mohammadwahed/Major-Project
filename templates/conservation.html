THE IUCN RED LIST FRAMEWORK

Understanding the labels

LC

Least Concern

Species assessed as widespread or abundant and not currently qualifying for a threatened category.

NT

Near Threatened

Species close to qualifying for a threatened category or likely to do so in the near future.

VU

Vulnerable

Species facing a high risk of extinction in the wild.

EN

Endangered

Species facing a very high risk of extinction in the wild.

CR

Critically Endangered

Species facing an extremely high risk of extinction in the wild.

EW / EX

Extinct in the Wild / Extinct

EW means survival only in cultivation, captivity, or outside the former range; EX means no reasonable doubt the last individual has died.

These are simplified educational summaries of IUCN categories. Always consult the current IUCN Red List assessment for a species-specific status.

WHAT WILDLIFE IS UP AGAINST

Pressure on the planet

01

Habitat loss

Forests, wetlands, grasslands, and other habitats are altered or fragmented by land-use change and development.

02

Poaching & illegal trade

Illegal hunting and wildlife trade can reduce populations and disrupt ecosystems.

03

Climate change

Shifting temperatures, rainfall, and ocean conditions can change where species can survive.

04

Pollution

Plastic, chemicals, and other pollutants can harm wildlife and degrade ecosystems.

MAKE A DIFFERENCE

Care is a conservation tool.

Choose informed, practical actions that support wildlife and healthy habitats.

  • ↗ Keep a respectful distance from wild animals; never feed them.
  • ↗ Reduce single-use plastics and dispose of waste responsibly.
  • ↗ Support credible conservation groups and local habitat projects.
  • ↗ Choose responsibly sourced products and avoid wildlife souvenirs.
  • ↗ Learn from trusted scientific and conservation organisations.
