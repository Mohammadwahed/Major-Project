ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology with
a little more purpose.

AI-Based Wildlife Learning Assistant is an educational web application designed to make wildlife learning engaging and accessible. It brings together a species directory, an AI-powered question-and-answer experience, and interactive learning activities.

The project demonstrates how modern web development and artificial intelligence can support biodiversity awareness. It is designed as a learning tool—not a substitute for professional wildlife identification or scientific advice.
